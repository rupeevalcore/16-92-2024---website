# Business & Marketing Guide

## Business Model
- **Core Offering:** Financial education workshops.
- **Value Proposition:** "Finance Made Simple. Life Made Smarter."
- **Target Audience:** Beginners, students, and early-career professionals seeking financial literacy.
- **Formats:**
  - Physical workshops in Chennai
  - Online sessions (Nationwide)

## Pricing Strategy
- **Group Sessions:** ₹499.00 per participant.
- **Strategy:** Affordable entry-level pricing to maximize accessibility and build trust.
- **Messaging:** "Starting from ₹499" to indicate value.

## Key Metrics
- **Traffic:** Website visitors (New vs. Returning).
- **Engagement:** Time on site, bounce rate.
- **Conversion:** Clicks on "Register for Workshop" and "WhatsApp Us".

## Brand Identity
- **Visual Style:** Modern, premium, trustworthy (Dark theme with blue/purple).
- **Tone of Voice:** Accessible, encouraging, educational, non-intimidating.
- **Key Message:** Practical skills you can use immediately.

## Marketing Channels
- **Digital:**
  - Website (SEO optimized)
  - Social Media (Instagram/LinkedIn)
  - Direct Messaging (WhatsApp)
- **Offline:**
  - College seminars
  - Community center partnerships

## Customer Journey
1.  **Awareness:** Discovers via social media or search.
2.  **Consideration:** Visits website, reviews curriculum and pricing.
3.  **Decision:** Clicks "Register" or contacts via WhatsApp.
4.  **Retention:** Attends workshop, potentially joins advanced sessions.
